Why the middle? Every comparison binary search makes throws away half of what is left. Half, then half of that, then half of that. The question “how many halvings until only one box is left?” is exactly what a logarithm answers.

What happens when the list gets big

Linear search's worst case is the list size. Binary search's worst case is how many times you can halve the list before one item is left. Watch the right-hand column barely move.

Read the last two rows Going from a thousand items to a million — a thousand times more data — costs binary search ten more comparisons. That is the whole reason anybody sorts anything.
